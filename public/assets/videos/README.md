# Product videos

- `harness.mp4` — the main Zeqou Harness promo video. It plays inside the
  Harness product block on the home page, the Apps catalogue and at the
  top of the Harness page (`HarnessPreview` component).
- `harness-extra.mp4` — a second cut. It plays in the "Another look"
  section further down the Harness page.

Both play muted, inline, autoplay, loop and lazy-load so they never
block the initial render.

Recommended encoding for fast loading:

- H.264, 1080p max, ~3–6 Mbps
- No audio track (the videos play muted)
